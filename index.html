Saturday 9 Jan: drive to the hotel, then train to the race

Morning

Drive to the hotel, leave the bags and the car there, then go to the race by bus, train and shuttle. You can't park in Adelboden, and this way you come straight back to the hotel with no car to fetch.

Staying for the party? The car stays at the hotel, so nobody has to drive. Return shuttles run until 23:00. The last train is 23:01 Frutigen → Spiez, then a taxi.
