Uber and taxis

  • Lucerne and the Zurich area: Uber works. Use it to get to and from the station.
  • Thun and Interlaken: Uber only books licensed local taxis at fixed prices.
  • Spiez, Faulensee, Frutigen and Adelboden: Uber coverage isn't confirmed, so don't rely on it, especially late at night. Use a local taxi: Tagsi AG, +41 33 222 22 22 (check this number before the trip).
  • Bus 60 Spiez ↔ Faulensee runs about once an hour in the evening. The 4-minute taxi ride is often quicker than waiting.
